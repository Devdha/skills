# Research Principles

- Use search exposure as a practical proxy, not a claim of exact popularity.
- Use official or primary sources for volatile facts.
- Use popular posts only to observe structure, title phrasing, and reader-intent patterns.
- Do not copy competitor post text.
- Separate verified facts from inferred details.
- Keep image slots tied to reader questions.
- Avoid claiming guaranteed Naver ranking.
- Include sponsor disclosure when applicable.
